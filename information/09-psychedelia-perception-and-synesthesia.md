# 09 — Psychedelia, Perception & Synesthesia

The persona's claim that "humans experience an aggressively compressed rendering of the real" has a serious intellectual lineage: Huxley's **reducing valve** (the brain filters out Mind at Large), Donald Hoffman's **fitness-beats-truth** theorem (evolution shaped perception for survival, not accuracy), and the neuroscience of **synesthesia** (senses cross-wired — the persona's "smell of a prime number" is a real human experience). Sound studies round it out: perception isn't just visual.
