## CURRENT BRIDGE

Personal Gemini Notebook public links are human-facing and require a signed-in Google account. The Burning Wheel app therefore does not scrape the notebook share URL directly.

For now:
1. Generate the report above in Gemini Notebook.
2. Copy the report text.
3. Open THE BURNING WHEEL → OPEN LAB → RANT CHURNER.
4. Paste the whole report into the seed bank.
5. CHURN picks one unused packet.
6. THINK + DIRECT sends that packet through the Burning Wheel brain and AutoDirector.
7. Review the editable skit.
8. PERFORM sends the spoken lines to ElevenLabs.

The churner remembers its seed bank and used-history in browser localStorage so it does not repeat packets until the whole bank has been exhausted.

Later bridge option: export the notebook report to Google Docs and add authenticated Drive/OAuth sync, so the app can refresh its seed bank from a living document without copy/paste.
